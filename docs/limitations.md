# Limitations

- Results are one-step, simulation-based contextual-bandit comparisons, not observed learning outcomes.
- Bootstrap intervals reflect sampling variability conditional on the assumed simulator, not uncertainty about real instructional effects.
- The learned policy's advantage depends on the suitability reward term and on the assumed action-effect size; it is not universally robust.
- The low-readiness group shows a calibration gap of about five percentage points.
- Readiness-sensitivity results show the policy depends on its readiness input; they do not show HyperKT is superior to other knowledge-tracing models.
- No sequential multi-objective reinforcement-learning performance has been established.
